01—03

Three small steps.

macOS will walk you through the install.

  1. 01

    Download and open Forge-v10.6.4-macos.dmg, then drag Forge into Applications.

  2. 02

    Because the app is unsigned, right-click (or Control-click) Forge in Applications and choose Open the first time.

  3. 03

    Click Open on macOS Gatekeeper’s confirmation prompt to launch Forge.

ⓘ Forge is community-developed and unsigned. Right-clicking and selecting Open the first time bypasses Gatekeeper safely.
